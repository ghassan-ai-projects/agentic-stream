# Visual guide quality bar

Every item is checked in a real browser (and, for the film, in the rendered
frames) before the visual guide is handed off. A failed item blocks the
handoff; the bar is never loosened to get a page green.

## Truth

- **T1. Every number is real.** Counts, values, versions, scores, digests and
  timings shown in the guide come from `src/data/run-data.json`, which
  `tools/extract-run-data.mjs` builds from the recorded joined run
  (`round-007-simulator-pass2`). No number is typed into a page by hand.
- **T2. Every claim has an owner.** Each station names the module that owns
  it, the invariants it protects and the reason it exists. Claims come from
  `documentation/` and `AGENTS.md`, not from memory.
- **T3. Proven versus designed is labelled.** The simulator run proves the
  LED path (`set_indicator`, R1). The fan path (`select_thermal_mode`, R2,
  human approval) is shown as the designed bench path and labelled so. The
  bench and the gateway are labelled as emulated: a simulator played the sensor
  and a device emulator answered commands. Illustrative moments (a malformed
  input, a late reading, an intent outside the catalog) say so on screen.

## Visual

- **V1. No overlap.** No label, caption or panel overlaps another, at
  1920x1080 and 1280x720, in every chapter. Checked by screenshot.
- **V2. Legible.** Caption text at least 26 px in the film; panel text at
  least 14 px in the world; contrast at least 4.5:1.
- **V3. One visual language.** One colour per plane (evidence, stream,
  cognition, governance, effect) across the 3D world, the labs and the film.
- **V4. Motion explains.** Every animation shows a mechanism (order, gate,
  immutability, fence). Nothing moves only for decoration.
- **V5. No console errors or warnings** on any page.

## Behaviour

- **B1. Deterministic.** The scene is a pure function of time: seeking to the
  same `t` twice yields identical pixels. This is what makes the film
  reproducible.
- **B2. Works offline from `file://`.** No network request, no CDN, no module
  that Chrome blocks on `file://`.
- **B3. Controls.** Play/pause, scrub, chapter jump, speed, free orbit with
  "resume tour", keyboard (space, arrows, 1-9). Reduced-motion users get a
  static, steppable view.
- **B4. Responsive.** Labs read at 390 px width without horizontal scroll;
  the world degrades to a vertical stage plus controls on narrow screens.

## Film

- **F1. 4 to 5 minutes**, 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC. The film is no longer
  held to two minutes: it gives each step of the data flow its own sentence and
  its own beat (watermark, late data, hysteresis, provisional and on-time
  versions, coalescing, rebinding, the catalog check, the three answers), with
  time to read every label. Under 4 minutes the mechanisms blur again; over 5 it
  stops being a single sitting.
- **F2. Narration and picture are locked.** Every sentence is synthesized and
  measured on its own, and every scene beat, label and camera move is scheduled
  on those measured cues (`cue()` and `word()` in `src/timeline.js`), never on a
  guessed offset. Counters change on the sentence that names them.
- **F3. Captions** burned in and shipped as `.vtt`.
- **F4. Audio** peaks below -1 dBFS, no clipping, no silence longer than
  1.5 s inside a chapter.

## Verification record

Checked on the final build with the tools in `tools/`.

| Item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| T1 numbers from the run | Pages read `src/data/run-data.json`, generated by `extract-run-data.mjs` (now also the first event's envelope: id, source, event time, arrival time) |
| T3 proven versus designed | Bench and gateway labelled emulated; R2 fan path labelled designed, not in this run; malformed input, late reading and refused intent labelled illustrative |
| V1 no overlap | `audit.mjs`: 0 issues in film mode at 1920x1080 and 1280x720 (every 0.5 s), and interactive at 1440x900 and 1280x720 (every 1 s). A label with no free spot is now hidden rather than overlapped |
| V5 console | No errors or warnings on the world, home and four labs (only cancelled media preloads when the headless browser closes) |
| B1 deterministic | `determinism.mjs`: SAME at 5, 30, 60, 90.5, 110, 119, 150, 175.5, 200, 230, 247.5 and 268 s |
| B2 offline | Every page loads from `file://` with no network request |
| B3 controls | `ui-test.mjs`: 13 of 13 checks pass (play, pause, chapter jump, explore, panel, deep link) |
| B4 responsive | Labs and home have no horizontal overflow at 390 px; the lab pictures scroll inside their card |
| Performance | `perf.mjs`: 4 to 12 ms per frame at 1440x900 @2x |
| F1 film | `film/agentic-stream-explained.mp4`: 270.3 s (4 min 30 s), 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, 58 MB |
| F2 narration locked | 45 sentence cues measured one by one; every beat, label and camera move is placed on a cue |
| F3 captions | Burned in; `film/captions.vtt` also shipped, one cue per spoken sentence |
| F4 audio | Peak -4.7 dBFS; every silence of 1.5 s or longer falls on a chapter boundary, none inside a chapter |

Known limits: on small screens, or with the guide panel open, the label layout
hides a lower-priority label when it has no free spot, so some notes disappear
below 1280 px. Word-level beats are placed by character position inside a
measured sentence, so they can land a syllable early or late.
The narration is ElevenLabs `eleven-v4` (voice `george`) through OpenRouter; its
levels and pauses are checked by tool, its sound by ear.
