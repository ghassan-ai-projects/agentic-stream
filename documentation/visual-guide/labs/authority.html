Lab 4 · Governance and effect planes

A proposal is not permission, and "OK" is not proof

Send a proposed action down the real path: validator, policy, outbox and dispatch, device, verification. Flip the conditions of the world and watch where it stops, what record is written, and what a lost reply does.

Reading the picture

Gates, records, outcome

Each gate either passes the proposal or stops it and writes a durable record with a reason. The chips under the picture are those records, in order.

Key idea

Check again, right before acting

Policy is evaluated against current state at dispatch. A superseded intent, an operator stop or a new runtime owner can turn an earlier yes into a no.

Why it matters

Unknown is a real answer

If a reply is lost, the system records an unknown outcome and reconciles with evidence. A stable idempotency key means a retry cannot cause a second effect.

The gate order, the risk classes (R0, R1, R2), the human approval rule for R2, and the three answers (receipt, result, query_state) follow the design and the recorded run. The failure switches are what-ifs; the recorded run passed every gate.
